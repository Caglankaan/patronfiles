Privacy Policy

Patron Files Publisher · Last updated November 9, 2026

Patron Files Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Patron Files channel to upload videos to that channel's own YouTube account. It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the Patron Files channel and to read basic information about that channel (for example, its name and its uploaded videos) to confirm uploads.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. It does not access viewers' data, comments' authors or any account other than the channel owner's.

Storage of credentials

The authorization token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and is used only to upload to that channel. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own terms apply: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
